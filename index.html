From Grains, Peels & Skins to Nutrition

Vitapeel is an agri-food innovation concept focused on creating nutritional flour by combining grains with responsibly processed plant-based peels and skins.

Our vision is to create useful food ingredients while encouraging better utilization of agricultural resources.

Our vision: Make nutritious food innovation more sustainable, accessible and resource-conscious.

Connecting Agriculture with Nutrition

Agriculture produces an enormous variety of grains, fruits and vegetables. Vitapeel explores ways to create additional value from selected parts of these crops.

Our long-term objective is to build a bridge between farmers, food processing and consumers.

  • Innovative flour formulations
  • Responsible ingredient utilization
